Privacy notice.

Last updated: September 2026

CHFR LDN ("CHFR", "we") provides private chauffeur services in London and nationwide. This notice explains what personal data we collect when you make a booking request, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

When you submit a booking request we collect only what is needed to arrange your journey:

  • Your name, mobile number and email address.
  • Pickup location, destination, date, pickup time, passenger and luggage numbers.
  • Journey type, preferred vehicle, flight number and any special requests you choose to give us.
  • Technical information about the submission: the browser you used, and a one-way cryptographic hash of your IP address used solely to detect automated abuse. We do not store your IP address itself.

What we never collect

CHFR does not request card or payment details online. No payment information is ever entered into, transmitted by, or stored on this website. Pricing is agreed with you directly by a CHFR concierge, and payment is arranged separately.

Why we hold it

We process your details to respond to your booking request, arrange and deliver the journey, and keep accurate records of the bookings we have handled. Our lawful bases are the performance of a contract with you (arranging your journey) and our legitimate interests in operating a chauffeur business and preventing fraudulent or automated misuse of the booking form.

Who can see it

Your details are visible to authorised CHFR staff through a private, password-protected operations dashboard. Every view is behind authentication and every change is recorded in an audit log. We use a small number of processors to run the service — an email provider to send booking confirmations, a spreadsheet service where a live operational sheet is in use, and a self-hosted WhatsApp gateway for internal notifications. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Booking records are retained while they are needed for the journey and for our business and accounting records afterwards. Records that are no longer needed are archived and then deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased, and to object to or restrict our processing of it. To exercise any of these rights, email us and quote your booking reference if you have one. We will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

The public CHFR website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. A single essential cookie is used by the private staff dashboard to keep signed-in staff authenticated; it is never set for ordinary visitors.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact CHFR LDN at CHFRLONDON@GMAIL.COM.
